标题：Construct Validity

~~~markdown
**英文 / 搜索词**

- construct validity
- validity of operationalization
- measurement validity

**类型**

- 正式理论 / 学术概念

**一句话解释**

Construct validity asks whether a measure or experiment really represents the abstract construct it claims to measure.

**核心理解**

The construct is the idea being studied; the operationalization is how the study makes that idea observable. A measure can be consistent and precise while still measuring the wrong thing. Construct validity therefore checks the relationship between the intended concept, the chosen indicators, and the conclusions drawn from them.

**例子 / 案例**

- If a study claims to measure “art understanding” using only recall of visual details, it may be measuring memory or description ability instead of interpretation.

**使用边界**

- Construct validity does not prove that a theory is true.
- It asks whether the chosen research operation supports the meaning assigned to the construct.

**适用场景**

- Reviewing an experiment, survey, prototype feedback form, rubric, or user-research metric.
- Asking whether a convenient observable variable is standing in for a richer concept.

**我的理解 / 以后怎么用**

When a project claims to measure a complex human experience, ask: “What construct do we intend, what did we actually observe, and what else could explain the result?”
~~~
