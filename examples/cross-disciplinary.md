标题：AI as Experimental Variable

~~~markdown
**英文 / 搜索词**

- AI as experimental variable
- AI-mediated interpretation
- interpretive mediation

**类型**

- 跨学科或工作性概念

**术语性质**

- This record uses AI as experimental variable as a cross-disciplinary working label, not as one universally standardized theory.

**一句话解释**

The label treats AI not only as a tool used in a study but also as a factor that can change the interaction, interpretation, behavior, and results being studied.

**核心理解**

If an AI system selects, summarizes, recommends, frames, or prompts, it participates in the conditions of the encounter. The research object is therefore not only the user's response to an artwork or task; it may also include how the AI's framing shaped that response.

**例子 / 案例**

- An exhibition prototype asks visitors about experience, time, purpose, emotion, and interest, then generates personalized guidance.
- If the system describes one artist's work while asking another artist to evaluate the description, the error is a technical failure, but it also exposes the system's role in assigning authorship and interpretive authority.

**使用边界**

- Treating AI as a variable does not mean assigning it human intention.
- The label should be operationalized: specify which AI behavior is being varied or observed.

**适用场景**

- AI-mediated art interpretation.
- Human-computer interaction studies.
- Prototype evaluation.
- Research on recommendation, framing, authorship, or viewer agency.

**我的理解 / 以后怎么用**

When evaluating an AI “helper,” ask what it changes in the situation: input selection, language, attention, interpretation, agency, or the evidence used to judge success.
~~~
