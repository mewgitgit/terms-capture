标题：Terms Capture Workflow

~~~markdown
**英文 / 搜索词**

- adaptive knowledge capture
- retrieval-oriented notes
- terms capture workflow

**类型**

- 方法 / 框架

**一句话解释**

Terms Capture is a method for turning a concept or selected discussion into a short record that supports future recognition, search, and use.

**核心理解**

The method does not begin with a fixed form. It first identifies what kind of material is being saved, then chooses the smallest structure that preserves meaning. A useful record moves from label and definition to mechanism, example, boundary, and future action.

**怎么使用**

- Identify the item and the reason it matters.
- Choose a shape: vocabulary, method, theory, working concept, source, or conversation.
- Write one clear explanation.
- Add the most useful mechanism, contrast, or example.
- Mark uncertainty or boundaries.
- End with a recognition cue or personal application.

**例子 / 案例**

- A method record needs a sequence and worked example.
- A conversation record needs the question, reasoning, decision, and open question.
- A formal concept record needs disciplinary status and boundary.

**使用边界**

- Do not force every record into every available section.
- Do not confuse a copy-ready archive with proof that the content is correct.

**适用场景**

- Research reading.
- Project vocabulary.
- Theory review.
- Preserving a useful reasoning chain from chat.

**我的理解 / 以后怎么用**

Use the workflow when a discussion produces a concept worth finding again, not when I only need a one-time explanation.
~~~
