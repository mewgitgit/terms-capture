标题：From Description Accuracy to Interpretive Authority

~~~markdown
**英文 / 搜索词**

- interpretive authority
- AI-mediated art interpretation
- viewer agency
- model bias
- construct validity

**类型**

- 聊天主题

**一句话解释**

The discussion moved from asking whether AI describes art accurately to asking who gets to frame meaning when AI mediates the viewer's encounter with a work.

**核心理解**

An exhibition prototype collected visitor variables and generated personalized guidance, recommendations, pairings, “Why Together,” and “Look For This” prompts. A wrong artist-specific page revealed a direct data-linking failure, but the deeper issue was structural: the system appeared to assume that more personalization and descriptive language automatically improve understanding.

The discussion therefore connected subjectivity, authorship, viewer agency, interpretive authority, construct validity, model bias, and AI as experimental variable. AI may be a tool, a medium, or an interpretive participant, depending on what it selects and frames.

**例子 / 案例**

- A page presents one artist's work but asks another artist whether the description captures their work.
- Descriptions of the actual work remain compressed summaries of visual elements and formal features.

**结论**

- Technical accuracy matters, but it does not settle the question of interpretive legitimacy.
- A system can be descriptively plausible while still narrowing the viewer's agency or misrepresenting authorship.
- Evaluation should test the construct being claimed: understanding, recall, interpretation, agency, or satisfaction are not interchangeable.

**未解决问题**

- When does AI become an interpretive subject rather than a delivery mechanism?
- How should artist intention, viewer interpretation, and model-generated framing be related?
- What evaluation design can distinguish helpful mediation from imposed interpretation?

**我的理解 / 以后怎么用**

When reviewing AI art tools, evaluate both output accuracy and the system's power to select, organize, and authorize meaning.
~~~
